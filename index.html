A Thrilling 3D Puzzle Experience Awaits - Match, Sort, and Conquer!

Funfair Match 3D is a visually stunning, brain-boosting puzzle game designed to challenge your memory, focus, and strategic skills in a highly engaging way. Each level presents exciting 3D objects that must be sorted and matched, keeping you hooked for hours!

Advantages

  • Beautiful 3D Object Design:

    Every level introduces new interactive elements

  • Brain Training & Relaxation:

    A perfect mix of strategy and stress relief

  • Compete with Players Worldwide:

    Climb the leaderboard and show off your skills

  • Regular Updates & Events: 

    Stay entertained with limited-time challenges and special seasonal levels

Benefits

  • A Fun Yet Challenging Puzzle Game - Easy to learn, yet increasingly difficult as you progress!

  • Brain-Stimulating Mechanics - Helps improve memory, logic, and problem-solving skills.

  • Multiple Game Modes - Play classic match-3, timed challenges, and mini-games for a diverse experience.

  • Earn Free Rewards Daily - Log in every day for coins, boosters, and surprise gifts!

  • Infinite Replayability - With hundreds of levels and frequent new updates, the fun never stops.

About us

  • At Sequel Games Inc., we specialize in creating interactive, visually captivating puzzle games that provide both entertainment and cognitive training.
  • With Funfair Match 3D, we wanted to bring a fresh approach to match-3 games, combining stunning 3D graphics, immersive gameplay, and strategic puzzle-solving. We listen to our players, constantly rolling out new features, exciting events, and challenging levels to ensure that the game remains fresh and engaging.
  • We invite you to step into the magical world of Funfair Match 3D and embark on a thrilling puzzle adventure where your logic, memory, and reflexes will be put to the test!
